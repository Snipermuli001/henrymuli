RADIO QUICK GUIDE

Find and play stations from locations around the world.

  1. 1Choose a location. Click Location to open the map, then search for or select any location worldwide.
  2. 2Confirm it. Click the blue Confirm button on the map.
  3. 3Browse stations. Open the drop-down list to see radio stations available in that area.
  4. 4Start listening. Choose a station and click Start.
  5. 5Give it a moment. Wait a few seconds for the stream to initialize, then enjoy!

Tip: You can drag the glowing ? button anywhere on the screen. Tap it again whenever you need help.
